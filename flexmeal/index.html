How a flexible meal fits into a normal week

FlexMeal follows one loop: plan, enjoy, log, understand, carry on. None of it asks you to make up for anything.

  1. Plan

    Put Friday’s pizza in the Planner. You get the day’s forecast, a reminder if you want one, and a “did you have it?” afterwards.

  2. Enjoy

    Eat the meal. That’s the whole step.

  3. Log

    Pick a category and a portion. A common meal takes about 15 seconds, and everything else is optional.

  4. Understand

    Over a few weeks, Insights shows which days and which foods come up most, and how much was planned.

  5. Continue normally

    The next meal is a regular one. If you fancy moving, FlexMeal suggests something that fits your day. It’s never owed.

Check in before scrolling

Choose the apps you open on autopilot, and FlexMeal asks for a quick check-in before they open. Screen Time keeps your choices private, even from FlexMeal.

Apple Health, read-only

Steps, workouts and active energy show up as context, never as a target. FlexMeal never writes to Health, and works fine without it.

Some words never show up in FlexMeal

Every line of text in the app is checked for guilt and payback language before it ships. A flexible meal is information, not a failure.

  • Cheat meal

    Flexible meal logged ✓

  • Burn it off

    Fancy some movement today? Pick whatever fits your day.

  • Earn your food

    Hope you enjoyed it.

  • You blew it this week

    Balance is built over time, not in one meal.

  • Back on track tomorrow

    Plans changed? No problem.

No account. No FlexMeal server.

What you log is yours. It stays on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. We can’t see it.
