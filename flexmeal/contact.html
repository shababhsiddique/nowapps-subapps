Common questions

How do I move my meals to a new iPhone?

Turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings ▸ Account on your current device. On the new device, sign in with the same Apple Account, install FlexMeal and choose Restore from iCloud when it's offered. You can also use Export Data to keep your own copy.

Apple Health shows no data in FlexMeal

Open the iOS Settings app ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Health ▸ FlexMeal and turn on the categories you want to share. FlexMeal only reads from Health. Meal logging works without it.

Can I hide calories?

Yes. Calories are always optional. Turn them off in Settings ▸ Preferences.

The Planner always shows a sun

Daily weather needs location access (While Using the App) and an internet connection. Without them, the Planner shows the sun.

How do I stop sending crash and usage data?

Settings ▸ Privacy & Data ▸ turn off Share crash & usage data.

How do I export or delete everything?

Settings ▸ Privacy & Data ▸ Your Data. Export Data creates JSON and CSV files. Delete All Data removes everything from this device and, if you choose, from iCloud.

How do I turn off Check-in Before Scrolling?

Turn it off in FlexMeal's Settings. You can also remove FlexMeal's Screen Time access in the iOS Settings app ▸ Screen Time.
