Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

The short version. FlexMeal has no accounts and no FlexMeal server. Your meals, plans and profile stay on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. We can't see that data. Apple Health data is only read on your device. It is never stored by FlexMeal, sent anywhere or used for ads. FlexMeal sends a few things off your device, all listed below: anonymous crash and usage data (you can turn this off), meal photos you choose to scan with the AI check, and your approximate location for the Planner weather.

This policy explains how the FlexMeal app for iPhone and iPad ("FlexMeal", "the app") handles your information. FlexMeal is made by Shabab Haider Siddique ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact contact.appsnow@gmail.com.

1. Data that stays on your device

These are created by you and stored in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Flexible meals you log or plan: name, category, time, notes, optional calorie estimates and optional photos.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: name, avatar, birth year, height, weight, sex, activity level and goals.
  • Workouts you log by hand, your workout preferences and app settings.
  • Recent photo scans. These stay on this device only and are never synced.

We do not run a server that receives this data, and we have no way to access it.

2. iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on Sync with iCloud (Settings ▸ Account), your FlexMeal data, including meal photos, is stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit. Only your Apple Account can read that database. We, as the developer, cannot. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn sync off at any time, and you can delete the iCloud copy from Settings ▸ Privacy & Data ▸ Your Data.

3. Apple Health

If you allow it, FlexMeal reads these types from Apple Health: steps, workouts, exercise minutes, active energy, walking and running distance, cycling distance, weight and height. FlexMeal uses them to show your weekly activity and suggest movement that fits your day.

  • FlexMeal never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • Health data is read when needed and used on your device. FlexMeal does not save it in its own storage, so it is never synced to iCloud.
  • Health data is never sent to us, to analytics or crash reporting, to the AI food check, or to anyone else.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and never sold.

The app works without Health access. You can change access at any time in the iOS Settings app ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Health ▸ FlexMeal.

4. Crash and usage data (optional, on by default)

To find bugs and understand which features are used, FlexMeal uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect:

  • Crash reports: stack traces, device model, iOS version, app version and free memory and disk space at the time of the crash.
  • Performance data and app interaction events that Firebase records automatically, such as app opens, screen views and session length.
  • A random installation identifier created by Firebase. It is not your name, email or Apple Account, and it is not the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Your approximate region (country or city level), which Google derives from your IP address.

We do not send your meals, photos, profile, notes or Health data to Firebase. FlexMeal does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

To turn this off: Settings ▸ Privacy & Data ▸ Share crash & usage data. When it's off, FlexMeal stops sending analytics and crash reports.

Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. Analytics event data is kept for the retention period set in our Firebase project.

5. AI food check (optional)

When you scan a meal photo, FlexMeal first tries to recognise the food on your device. If the device isn't sure and you agree to the AI check, the photo is sent to Bitmesh (bitmesh.ai), which uses OpenAI and Qwen models to list the foods and estimate portions. The app asks for your consent before the first AI check.

  • Only the photo is sent. Your name, profile and Health data are not.
  • We don't keep a copy of the photo on any server. Bitmesh and its model providers process it under their own privacy policies.
  • Each request includes a Firebase App Check token. Apple's App Attest service uses this token to confirm the request comes from a genuine copy of FlexMeal. The token does not identify you.

Meal photos you take or choose without scanning are not sent anywhere. FlexMeal uses the camera only when you tap to take a photo. Choosing a photo uses Apple's photo picker, so FlexMeal sees only the photo you pick, not your library.

6. Weather in the Planner (optional)

If you allow location access, FlexMeal uses your approximate location while the app is open to ask Apple WeatherKit for the daily forecast. The location is used for that one request. FlexMeal does not store it, send it to analytics or send it anywhere else. Only the forecast symbols for each day are kept on the device, for up to 24 hours. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather. Apple's handling of the request is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Check-in Before Scrolling (optional)

If you turn on this feature, FlexMeal uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to ask for a quick FlexMeal check-in before the apps you choose open. Apple gives FlexMeal only opaque tokens for your choices, so FlexMeal can't see which apps or websites you picked or how you use them. Nothing about your app usage leaves your device.

8. Notifications

Reminders, such as planned-meal reminders and the weekly check-in, are scheduled on your device. FlexMeal does not send marketing push notifications.

9. Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings ▸ Privacy & Data ▸ Your Data ▸ Export Data creates JSON and CSV files that you can save or share.
  • Delete: Delete All Data on the same screen removes every meal, plan, scan, your profile and settings from this device. If sync is on, you can also delete the iCloud copy there.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device data. Your iCloud copy stays until you delete it in the app, or in the iOS Settings app ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage.
  • For data held by Firebase, turn off sharing (section 4) and contact us with your request. Because we have no account system, we may need help from you to identify the installation.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, UK or California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Most of your data is under your own control in the app, as described above. For anything else, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We rely on your consent for optional features (Health, AI check, location, crash and usage data) and on our legitimate interest in keeping the app reliable for crash reporting. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising.

11. Children

FlexMeal is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

12. International transfers and security

Google, Bitmesh and their model providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own safeguards. Data sent by the app is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On-device data is protected by iOS data protection, and iCloud data by Apple's encryption.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app or in the App Store release notes.
