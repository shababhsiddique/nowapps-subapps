Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2, 2026

Thank you for using DripRoom: AI Outfit Try-On ("the App", "we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use the App and how we handle it.

1. No Account Required

DripRoom: AI Outfit Try-On does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, or other contact details through normal use of the App unless you contact us directly.

2. Photos and Content You Provide

The App lets you upload portrait photos and clothing images to generate AI virtual try-on results. You may also save portraits, garments, and generated outfits locally in your on-device wardrobe.

To perform try-on generation, your selected images are transmitted securely over the internet to our AI processing partners for analysis and rendering. We use these images only to provide the try-on feature you request. We do not use your photos for advertising, and we do not sell your images to third parties. Your images are deleted from server as soon as operation completed.

Processing partners may temporarily retain submitted images only as needed to complete a request and in accordance with their own privacy and security practices.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your iPhone:

  • Portraits and clothing items in your wardrobe
  • Saved try-on results and outfit history
  • Free-tier and subscription credit usage counters
  • App preferences and onboarding state

You can delete portraits, clothing, and saved outfits from within the App at any time.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions and credit packs are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple may provide us with purchase and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features and credits.

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

We do not operate a user database or track you across other apps and websites. The App may use standard iOS diagnostics solely to keep the service reliable.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share image data only with service providers that help us deliver AI try-on features. We may disclose information if required by law.

If you share a try-on result using iOS share features, that sharing is under your control.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit. No method of transmission is completely secure. Please use caution when uploading personal photos.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe we have done so inadvertently.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Much of your data stays on your device — delete content in the App or email us for other requests.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
