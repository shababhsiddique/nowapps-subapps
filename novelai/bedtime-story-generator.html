What a story looks like

This is the start of Poppy and the Sleepy Moon, a short bedtime story of 357 words, about two minutes of reading aloud.

Poppy and the Sleepy Moon

Poppy had counted forty-one sheep, then a duck by mistake, and now she was wide awake. So she climbed onto the window seat and pushed the curtain aside.

The moon was yawning.

It was a very small yawn, half hidden behind a cloud, the way you yawn in class when you hope nobody sees. But Poppy saw.

Make the story theirs

  • Put your child in it. Make a character from one line, “a girl with a red raincoat who talks to puddles”, star it, and it appears in the story. On the free plan you can make two of your own.
  • Change what they don’t like. Tap Change it and say “make the duck a goose” or “shorter”. Every version is kept, so you can go back.
  • Carry on tomorrow. Keep going writes the next part in the same style. Or turn a favourite into a book with chapters and read one a night.
  • Print it. Export a book as a PDF and print it, or keep it on the iPad.

Stories that suit their age

Every story has an age group: Kids, Pre-teen, Teen or Adult. For Kids, Novel AI tells the AI to avoid romance, graphic violence and sensitive themes, in stories and in every other mode. These rules reduce unsuitable writing but can’t rule it out, so read along with young children, and report anything that isn’t right from the story’s ••• menu.

Stories stay on your device. Your child’s voice is turned into text by Apple’s speech recognition and no recording is kept. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Novel AI gives you 20 creations a day, and 30 on your first day, without paying. Premium, from $3.99 a week, removes the daily limit.

How long is a bedtime story?

Pick Short, Medium or Long. A Short story is a few minutes of reading; the example above is 357 words. Long stories come with Premium.

Can my child make the story with their voice?

Yes. Tap the mic and they can say the idea out loud. Nothing is sent until you tap the button to write the story, so you can check the words first.

Can I make bedtime stories in another language?

Yes. Novel AI works in 11 languages, including Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

Does it need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Stories are saved on your iPhone or iPad.
