How Book mode works

  1. Switch Create to BookSay or type the idea for the whole book. Pick a genre, an age group and a narrator style, and star any characters you want in it.
  2. Shape the chapter planNovel AI suggests the chapters with a title and a short summary each. Rename them, reorder them, add one or remove one before anything is written.
  3. Write the chaptersWrite them one at a time and read as you go, or with Premium tap Write all chapters and Novel AI writes the rest, even with the app closed, then tells you the book is ready.
  4. Export the bookShare it as a PDF to print or send anywhere, or as an EPUB that opens in Apple Books and Kindle, with a cover page if you want one.

Chapters that keep track of the story

When Novel AI writes a chapter, it works from the book’s title, its idea, the chapter plan and a summary of what happened in the chapter before. That is how names, places and promises carry through the book instead of being forgotten halfway.

At the end of a chapter you can add one more. Let the AI continue, or guide it: write what should happen next, or tap a quick idea such as a plot twist, a new character, a time skip or a final chapter, and pick how long the chapter should be.

Start from a story you already have

Any story Novel AI writes can become a book. Open it and tap Make it a book, and Novel AI plans chapters from it that you can shape before they are written. Covers come with every story and book; see some on the Novel AI home page.

Who owns the book

You do. As between you and us, you own the stories and books you create, including the PDF and EPUB files you export, as far as the law allows rights in AI-assisted writing. If you publish or sell a book, check that it doesn’t borrow from anyone else’s work. The terms of use have the details.

Questions

How many chapters can a book have?

Free books can have up to 3 written chapters. Premium books go up to 25 chapters.

Can I export my book to Kindle or Apple Books?

Yes. Export it as an EPUB with Premium, which opens in Apple Books and Kindle. Free exports are PDFs that end with a small “Made with Novel AI” line.

Can I change a chapter after it is written?

Yes. Ask for a change in your own words; each change is saved as a new version, so the earlier one is never lost.

Does each chapter cost a creation?

Yes. A chapter counts as one of your 20 free creations a day. Premium has no daily allowance.

Where is my book saved?

On your device. It isn’t synced or backed up by us, so export the books you want to keep.
