Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy covers the Novel AI app for iPhone and iPad and this website. In short: Novel AI has no user account, your stories stay on your device, and only the text needed to write a story is sent to our AI provider, without your name or Apple Account.

Who we are

Novel AI is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). The app is offered as a free app with an optional Premium subscription. Questions about this policy go to contact.appsnow@gmail.com.

What the app sends to write a story

Novel AI uses artificial intelligence to write stories, books, Play scenes, character replies, character profiles, translations and cover images. When you ask for one of these, the app sends the following to our AI service provider, Bitmesh, which passes it to the AI model providers it uses, such as OpenAI:

  • what you type or dictate (story and book ideas, chat messages, requests for changes), as text;
  • the details of the characters you pick, such as name, appearance, personality, backstory and way of speaking;
  • the story settings: genre, length, language, age group and narrator style;
  • for a story or chat in progress, a short automatic summary of it so far and the latest messages, so the result stays consistent;
  • for a book chapter, the book’s title, idea, chapter plan and a summary of the previous chapter.

We do not send your name, email address or Apple Account. Requests are identified only by the app’s key and a device-integrity token from Firebase App Check, not by who you are. Before your first AI request the app asks your permission to share this information with these providers; if you choose “Not now”, nothing is sent.

Bitmesh and the model providers process this information to produce the result, under their own terms. Please don’t put information you want to keep private, such as real addresses or health details, into a story or chat.

Voice input

When you tap the microphone, the app uses Apple’s speech recognition to turn your voice into text. Where your language supports it, this happens on your device. For other languages, Apple’s speech recognition service processes the audio under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We never receive or store voice recordings, and audio is never sent to our AI providers; only the text you choose to send is. You can turn off microphone and speech recognition access at any time in iOS Settings.

What stays on your device

  • Your stories, books, chats, characters and drafts. They are not uploaded to our servers, not synced between devices and not backed up by us, except as part of your own iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Exports. PDF and EPUB files are created on your device and go wherever you save or share them. We don’t receive a copy.
  • Reports. When you report an AI reply, the report is saved on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Your daily creation count for the free plan.
  • Notifications. Story reminders and “your book is ready” notices are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is involved.

You can delete a story or book from its ••• menu or from Drafts, a Talk chat from its ••• menu, and a character from Characters.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check what you are entitled to, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history with an anonymous app user ID.

Analytics and tracking

The current version of the app does not use an analytics or crash-reporting service, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and shows no ads. If this changes, we will update this policy before the version that changes it.

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Third-party services

  • Bitmesh, AI request routing
  • OpenAI, AI models: privacy policy
  • Firebase App Check by Google, device integrity: privacy information
  • RevenueCat, subscription status: privacy policy
  • Apple, purchases and speech recognition: privacy policy

When information may be disclosed

We hold very little information about you. What we do hold may be disclosed only:

  • when the law requires it, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to protect our rights or your safety or the safety of others, to investigate fraud, or to respond to a government request;
  • to service providers working on our behalf, who may not use it for anything else.

Children

Each story has an age group (Kids, Pre-teen, Teen or Adult) that limits what the AI may write. This is a content setting, not age verification. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the app has no account or sign-up. Young children should use the app with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Because there is no account and your content is stored on your device, deleting the app removes your stories and settings. We cannot look up or delete a particular person’s content held by the AI providers, since requests carry no identity, but if you have a privacy request, email us and we will help as far as we can, including contacting the provider. You can stop all data processing by the app by uninstalling it.

Security

Requests are sent over encrypted connections, and we use physical, electronic and procedural safeguards for the information we process. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains a feature. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.
