What is the difference between Story and Book?

Story writes a complete story in one go. Book plans chapters first, which you can change, then writes them one at a time.

Is my voice recorded?

No. Apple’s speech recognition turns it into text, on your device when your language supports it. Only that text is sent.

What counts as a creation?

Any AI result: a story, a change, a chapter, a Play scene, a Talk reply or a character. The count resets at midnight.

Where are my stories saved?

On your device only. They do not sync, so deleting the app deletes them. Export books you want to keep.

Is it safe for kids?

Kids, Pre-teen and Teen stories avoid romance, graphic violence and sensitive themes. Report anything that slips through from the ••• menu.
