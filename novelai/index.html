Example storyBedtime, for kids

Poppy and the Sleepy Moon

Poppy had counted forty-one sheep, then a duck by mistake, and now she was wide awake. So she climbed onto the window seat and pushed the curtain aside.

The moon was yawning.

It was a very small yawn, half hidden behind a cloud, the way you yawn in class when you hope nobody sees. But Poppy saw.

“Are you tired?” she whispered.

“A little,” said the moon, in a voice like a faraway bell. “I have been up since the dinosaurs.”

Poppy thought that sounded like far too long. “Doesn’t anyone put you to bed?”

The moon was quiet for a moment. “There are the stars, but they are always busy twinkling.”
