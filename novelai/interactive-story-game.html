Interactive story game where you choose what happens

Turn any story into a game you play as the hero. Each scene ends with three choices, or you type or say your own move, and Novel AI writes what happens next. Then talk to the characters in their own world.

Download Novel AI

Play and Talk are free within your 20 creations a day. Each scene and each reply counts as one.

How Play works

  1. Pick a story and a heroWrite a new story or open one from your Library, then tap Play as and choose a character to be.
  2. Choose, or make your own moveEvery scene ends with three choices. Tap one, or type or say what you do instead, and the story follows you.
  3. Reach the cliffhangerThe fifth scene ends the chapter on a cliffhanger. Tap Next chapter to carry on, or save it and come back later from In progress.

The bell stops. Somewhere below the tower, a door you sealed three hundred years ago swings open.

1. Go down to the door
2. Ring the bell again

One scene in a chapter may offer a Premium path, marked with a crown. Every other choice is free.

Talk to the characters

Open Talk to chat with a character inside their world: ask the knight why he left the capital, or the pirate what she misses about the sea. Replies are short and stay in character. Tap a topic to ask about their past, or tap Turn this into a story to make the conversation into a new story.

The characters are fictional AI characters, not real people, and the app always says so. Nothing they say is advice.

Make your own characters

Describe a character in one line, “a retired pirate who runs a bakery”, and Novel AI fills in a name, traits, a backstory and a way of talking. Star them to put them in a story, then play alongside them or talk to them. On the free plan you can make two characters of your own; Premium has no limit.

For every age group

Play and Talk follow the story’s age group. For Kids, Pre-teen and Teen, the AI is told to avoid romance, graphic violence and sensitive themes. If something isn’t right, report the reply from its ••• menu.

Questions

Is it like a choose-your-own-adventure book?

Yes, except the story is new every time and you are not limited to the choices: you can type or say your own move.

Can I play a story I already wrote?

Yes. Open any story and tap Play as, then pick the character you want to be.

How many scenes are in a chapter?

Five. The fifth scene ends the chapter on a cliffhanger, and you choose whether to carry on.

Are the characters real people?

No. They are fictional AI characters, and the app says so in every chat.

Where are my games saved?

In your Library under In progress, on your device. Tap Continue to pick up where you stopped.
