Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2025

Thank you for using Easy Caption ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use the App.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Easy Caption does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) from users. We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal details to use the features of the App.

2. Data Processing

The Easy Caption app works by generating automatic subtitles for videos you upload. The videos processed by the App remain private to you and are not shared, stored, or transferred to any third-party servers. All data processing is done locally on your device, ensuring your content remains under your control.

3. No Sharing of Personal Information

Since Easy Caption does not collect personal information, we do not share, sell, or rent any personal data to third parties. Your videos, subtitles, and any other content created within the App are not shared with third parties unless you choose to share them manually through external platforms.

4. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the content you upload and generate. However, please note that the security of data transmitted via the internet or stored on your device cannot be guaranteed 100%. We encourage users to take appropriate precautions when using the App, especially when handling sensitive content.

5. Third-Party Services

The Easy Caption App may use third-party services (such as video processing libraries or cloud storage solutions) to help facilitate the functioning of the App. However, these services do not collect or store any personal data from you.

Please review the privacy policies of any third-party services integrated into the App to understand their data practices.

6. Children's Privacy

The Easy Caption App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under the age of 13, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make updates, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact.appsnow@gmail.com.
