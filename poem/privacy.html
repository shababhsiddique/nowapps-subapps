In short: Poem Writer has no account. Your poems stay on your device. Only what is needed to write a poem, its cover or a translation is sent to our AI provider, without your name or Apple Account.

Who we are

Poem Writer is published by Bitmesh (“we”, “us”). The app is free to download with an optional Poem Writer Pro subscription. Questions about this policy go to contact.appsnow@gmail.com.

What is sent to write a poem

Poem Writer uses artificial intelligence to write poems, paint cover artwork and translate poems. When you ask for one of these, the app sends the following over an encrypted connection to our AI service, Bitmesh, which passes it to the AI model providers it uses, such as OpenAI:

  • for a poem: the topic or words you typed, and your choice of form, tone, length and language;
  • for cover artwork: the poem’s title and text, and the artwork style you chose;
  • for a translation: the poem’s title and text, its language, the language you want, and any mood you picked for the translation.

We do not send your name, email address, contacts, location or Apple Account. Requests are signed with the app’s own key, not with anything that identifies you. Bitmesh and the model providers process this information to produce the result, under their own terms.

Please don’t put information you want to keep private, such as addresses, phone numbers or health details, into a topic.

What stays on your device

  • Your poems and drafts. They are saved on your iPhone or iPad. They are not uploaded to our servers, not synced between devices and not backed up by us, except as part of your own iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Photos. If you set a poem over a photo of your own, you pick it with Apple’s photo picker. The photo is used on your device to make the image and is never sent to us or to our AI providers.
  • Saved and shared images. Artwork you save goes to your photo library, with your permission, and anything you share goes where you send it. We don’t receive a copy.
  • Your settings and counts, such as your last-used options, how many free poems and downloads you have used, and how long the app has been open, which decides when the app asks for an App Store rating.

Read aloud

Reading mode uses the text-to-speech voices built into iOS and iPadOS. The poem is spoken on your device. No audio is recorded, and nothing is sent to us to read a poem aloud.

On this website, the read-aloud sample uses your browser’s own speech feature. Depending on your browser and the voice it picks, the sample poem’s text may be processed by your browser’s maker to produce the speech.

Purchases

Poem Writer Pro is sold and charged by Apple. We never see your payment details. To check what you are entitled to, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history with an anonymous app user ID.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not use an analytics or crash-reporting service, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and shows no ads. If this changes, we will update this policy before the version that changes it.

This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It remembers the sample feeling you last picked in your browser’s local storage, which never leaves your device. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Third-party services

  • Bitmesh: AI request routing
  • OpenAI and other model providers used by Bitmesh: writing, artwork and translation (OpenAI privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat: subscription status (privacy policy)
  • Apple: purchases, photo picker and on-device voices (privacy policy)
  • Google Fonts: fonts on this website (privacy policy)

When information may be disclosed

We hold very little information about you. What we do hold may be disclosed only:

  • when the law requires it, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when we believe in good faith that it is necessary to protect our rights or your safety or the safety of others, to investigate fraud, or to respond to a government request;
  • to service providers working on our behalf, who may not use it for anything else.

Children

Poem Writer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app has no account or sign-up. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting information

Because there is no account and your poems are stored on your device, deleting a poem from Drafts, or deleting the app, removes it. We cannot look up a particular person’s requests held by the AI providers, since requests carry no identity, but if you have a privacy request, email us and we will help as far as we can. You can stop all processing by the app by uninstalling it.

Security

Requests are sent over encrypted connections, and we use physical, electronic and procedural safeguards for the information we process. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains a feature. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.
