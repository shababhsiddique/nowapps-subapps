What makes a love poem land

Most generated love poems fail the same way: they could be about anyone. Hearts, stars, forever. The ones people keep are specific. Poem Writer’s love poem form asks for intimacy, longing or devotion in particular detail rather than general sentiment, and speaks to the person directly where that helps.

You supply the detail. The app does the rest:

  • Name a moment, not a quality. Not “she’s kind”, but “she leaves the hall light on for me”.
  • Include one thing only they would recognise. A joke, a street, a song you both sing wrong.
  • Choose how it should feel. Romantic, playful, grateful, nostalgic, or wistful if you’re apart.

Two love poems from one kind of detail

Written for this page to show the idea. The app writes a new poem each time you ask.

Hall Light

Romantic · Free verse

You leave the hall light on for me,
though you are asleep by ten
and I am never home by ten.

It burns the whole night through the glass,
a small sun nobody asked for,
and I find my keys by it.

I used to think love was a word
you had to say out loud.
Now I think it is a switch
you choose not to touch.

Wrong Way

Playful · Rhymed quatrains

You steal the blanket every night,
you hum the wrong words to our song,
you swear the map is held up right
and drive us forty minutes wrong.

I’d take the cold, the wrong-way miles,
the chorus mangled into mush,
for one of those sideways smiles
you give me when you tell me hush.

Write one in the app

  1. Say who and what. “For Sam, ten years married, who still dances in the kitchen.”
  2. Choose the form and tone. Love poem and Romantic is the easy start; 30 forms and 33 tones in all.
  3. Make it theirs. Translate it into their language, give it a painted cover or set it over a photo of the two of you.
  4. Send it, or read it to them. Share the poem as text or an image, or play it in reading mode, where each line lights as it is spoken.
