Two kinds of sonnet

Shakespearean (English)

Three quatrains and a closing couplet, rhymed ABAB CDCD EFEF GG. The quatrains build an argument, the turn arrives at line 9, and the couplet lands it. Poem Writer sets it out as four stanzas of 4, 4, 4 and 2 lines.

Petrarchan (Italian)

An octave of eight lines rhymed ABBAABBA, then a sestet of six, usually CDECDE or CDCDCD. The octave sets out a problem; the volta at line 9 opens the answer. Poem Writer sets it out as two stanzas, 8 and 6.

Both are written in iambic pentameter: ten syllables a line, five stresses, da-DUM five times.

The Piano in the Hall

Reflective · Shakespearean sonnet

The old piano waits beside the stair,
its ivory gone yellow as the moon;
nobody plays it now, but it is there,
and holds, I think, the shape of every tune.

My mother played it on the winter nights
while snow came down against the window frame;
she’d hum a little flat beneath the lights
and played without the music, all the same.

Now I am grown, and never learned to play;
my fingers stumble where hers knew the score.
But some nights, when the house has gone to grey,
I lift the lid and press one key, no more,

and one note fills the hall the way she would,
as if the house remembers. And it should.

Stair, there; moon, tune: that is the A and B of the first quatrain. Each quatrain brings two new rhymes, “Now I am grown” is the turn at line 9, and the couplet closes on would and should.

How Poem Writer keeps the form

Language models are good at sounding like a sonnet and bad at counting to fourteen. Before a sonnet reaches you, Poem Writer checks it:

  • exactly 14 lines, in the right stanzas for the kind you chose;
  • line ends that follow the rhyme scheme;
  • lines near the ten syllables of pentameter.

Anything that breaks the form is sent back as a correction and rewritten. Rhymes and syllables are judged by rule of thumb, so a single slant rhyme or a long line can pass rather than throwing away a good poem. Rhyme and syllable checks run on English sonnets; in other languages the app keeps the lines and stanzas.

Questions

Is the sonnet generator free?

The Shakespearean sonnet is in Poem Writer’s free plan, which includes a limited number of poems. The Petrarchan sonnet, villanelle, sestina and the rest of the 30 forms come with Poem Writer Pro.

Can I write a love sonnet?

Yes. Choose a sonnet as the form and Romantic as the tone, and put the person and a detail only you know in the subject. The love poem generator page has more on writing for someone.

Is it real iambic pentameter?

The app asks for it and checks that lines sit near ten syllables. Stress is harder to measure than syllables, so read it aloud in the app’s reading mode and you’ll hear where it scans.
